✨ Over a Decade of Artistry

Welcome to Hand Art Gallery! I'm Yasmeen Khan, a passionate artist with over a decade of experience in the beautiful world of body art.

My specialties include vibrant face painting and traditional, intricate Indian henna designs (mehndi) applied to hands and various other parts of the body. Every design is crafted with precision, love, and a deep appreciation for cultural artistry.

Over the years, I’ve had the absolute joy of bringing smiles to countless kids' birthday parties, adding elegance to weddings, and bringing life to fairs and community events. Whether it's a fierce tiger mask, a delicate unicorn, or bridal henna, I love what I do and enjoy making every occasion unforgettable.

Let me add a splash of color and tradition to your next special event!
